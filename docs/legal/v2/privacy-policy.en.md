## 2. Summary

- We collect what is needed to run a carpooling platform: your account details, the
  journeys you search for, publish and book, messages and ratings, and — during a Trip —
  the Driver's location.
- Drivers additionally provide identity, licence and insurance photos, which only
  authorised VAYA staff can see.
- Other Members see your name, photo, ratings and (for Drivers) vehicle details. Your phone
  number is shown only to the other party once a Booking is accepted.
- We do not sell your data, show advertising, or use third-party analytics or advertising
  trackers.
- We use service providers (for example for SMS, maps, notifications and hosting), some
  of which are located outside [ACTION REQUIRED — CONFIRM: Tunisia / the EU].
- You can access, correct and delete your data. Account deletion is available in the app.

## 11. Automated decisions and profiling

We use automated processing in the following ways:

- **Search ranking and Stop proposals.** Rides are ordered by how well they fit your
  journey (distance to the Stops, detour, departure time). This does not decide whether
  you may use the service.
- **Contribution Range.** Calculated from route distance and estimated duration.
- **Trust level.** Calculated from your number of completed Trips, average rating and
  account age.
- **Recurring-journey suggestions.** Inferred from your history; you can dismiss them.
- **Automatic Trip progress.** Arrival at the pickup point, boarding and completion may be
  inferred from location data; Trips that remain open long after their expected end are
  closed automatically.
- **Automatic no-show classification.** When location data and Trip status clearly show
  that a party never came to the meeting point, the Platform may record a no-show. This has
  consequences for the absent Member: reliability points and a 1-star rating.

The last of these can significantly affect your reputation on the Platform. You have the
right to obtain human review, to express your point of view and to contest the decision by
contacting [ACTION REQUIRED — CONFIRM: support email] (see Terms, Article 10.5). VAYA does
not take decisions to suspend or close accounts by automated means alone; such decisions
are taken by a member of staff.

## 13. Deleting your account

- **How:** in the app, Profile → Terms & privacy → Delete account, or by writing to us.
- **When it is not possible:** while you have a pending or accepted Booking, or a Ride
  that is published, full or in progress. The app tells you what to cancel first. This
  avoids leaving another Member without a Driver or Passenger.
- **What happens immediately:** your name is replaced by a neutral placeholder; your phone
  number, email address, Google identifier and profile photo are removed; your Driver
  Verification photos and profile photo are deleted from storage; all your sessions are
  signed out. Deletion is permanent.

tracking is unavailable.
