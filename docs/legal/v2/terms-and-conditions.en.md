## Summary in plain language

This summary helps you read the Terms; it does not replace them.

- VAYA is an app that connects people who are already making a car journey (**Drivers**)
  with people who want to travel the same way (**Passengers**) so that they can **share
  the cost** of the journey. VAYA is not a transport company and does not drive anyone.
- Using VAYA is **free**. VAYA does not handle payments: Passengers pay their contribution
  directly to the Driver.
- The price per seat must stay within a range that VAYA calculates from the route. A
  Driver must never make a profit from a journey.
- Drivers must be verified by VAYA before they can publish a journey.
- If you cancel late or do not show up, your reliability record is affected. There are
  no financial penalties.
- You can delete your account at any time from the app.
- If VAYA restricts your account, we will tell you why and you can ask us to review the
  decision.

## 6. Cost-sharing — the Contribution

6.1. **VAYA is for sharing costs, not for earning money.** A Driver may only ask
Passengers to contribute to the actual costs of the journey (such as fuel, tolls, parking

use the Platform to carry on a commercial or professional passenger-transport activity
(such as taxi, louage or ride-hailing services).

6.2. **Contribution Range.** For each Ride the Platform calculates a recommended
Contribution per seat from the route's distance and estimated duration, using reference

Driver chooses a Contribution within that range. The Platform rejects any price outside
it. The Contribution shown on the Ride when a Passenger books is the amount agreed.

6.3. **Driver's responsibility for the total.** The Contribution Range applies **per seat**.

## 10. Cancellations, no-shows and reliability

10.1. Either party may cancel a Booking in the app and must select a reason. The
consequence depends on how long before the scheduled departure the cancellation happens:

| When the cancellation happens | Consequence for the cancelling Member |
|---|---|
| 24 hours or more before departure | None |
| Less than 24 hours but at least 30 minutes before departure | 1 reliability point |
| Less than 30 minutes before departure, or after departure | 3 reliability points |

10.2. **No-show.** If a Member does not turn up for a Booking, the other party may report
a no-show in the app from 15 minutes after the scheduled departure time. The Platform may
also classify a no-show automatically when location data and Trip status clearly show
that one party never came to the meeting point. A confirmed no-show adds 5 reliability
points to the absent Member and records a 1-star rating against them.

10.3. **Reliability points** are an internal record used by VAYA to identify repeated
unreliability. They are not shown to other Members. Repeated cancellations or no-shows may
lead to measures under Article 17.

10.4. **No financial penalties.** Because VAYA does not handle payments, cancellations and
no-shows have no financial consequences through the Platform. Any agreement between
Members about money already paid is between them.

10.5. **Contesting a no-show.** If you believe a no-show was recorded against you in error
(including an automatic classification), contact us at [ACTION REQUIRED — CONFIRM:
support email] within 14 days. A member of our team will review it and remove the
rating and points if the record was wrong.

10.6. **Changes to the thresholds.** We may adjust the time thresholds or points above.

10.7. **Ride cancellation by the Driver.** A Driver who cancels a Ride cancels the related
